The Costume
Book.

Notes for characters’ wardrobes, silhouettes, textures, and transformations.

Costumes should feel worn in, used, thrifted. Interior Clothing: Essentials: Thin, light, neutral colors showing skin and suggesting physicality and giving intimacy with characters. These are mainly rehearsal clothes with layers that bridge interior/exterior for scenes inside that aren't rehearsal scenes. Hightened styling of the same clothes for dance numbers. Exterior: Big, bulky, oversized, texture and controlled color: Dramatic silhouettes in cold exterior situations, ensuring warmth and contrasting visibility against brown/white(snow). Jonah and Crew wear mainly black clothing to contrast with the main cast. The black clothing is also a visual cue for the audience to identify them as a behind-the-scenes group. Use variation with: blue denim jeans, dark colored shirts/layers. For exteriors a wider allowance.
