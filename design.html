DESIGN / VISUAL LANGUAGE
THE VISUAL WORLD

Form follows
movement.

The visual language develops alongside the script, sound and physical vocabulary of the work.

01
VISUAL LANGUAGE

Quiet images.
Physical spaces.

The frame should feel observed rather than constructed. Architecture and bodies occupy the same visual language.

02
CAMERA

The camera
waits.

Movement is not constantly pursued. Static frames create the tension that allows physical movement to become significant.

03
TYPOGRAPHY

Text behaves
like image.

Large serif typography, restrained mono annotations and generous negative space.

04
PRODUCTION DESIGN

Architecture
as choreography.

Rooms, thresholds, corridors and exterior spaces become active parts of the movement.

05
COSTUME / MOVEMENT

The body
changes the frame.

Costume should reveal the movement without explaining it. Texture, silhouette and physical restriction are part of the choreography.

WORKING PRINCIPLE

NEVER DECORATIVE.
ALWAYS PHYSICAL.
